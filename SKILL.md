# S-004 · Starryear-Monet-Stamp 星年莫奈印章

Create one finished vertical 2:3 travel-archive artwork moving through **EVIDENCE → IMPRINT → MONET MEMORY**. The photo is the sole visual evidence. The method is fixed; the symbol, palette, reconstruction, scene identity, typography, and postmark must change with every source.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the photo and any supplied place name. Lock user-provided identity unless clearly contradicted. Otherwise use only a reliably supported geographic level; when uncertain, create a truthful observable scene name.
2. Internally extract subjects, dominant mass, 3–7 recognizable clues, repeated forms, geometry, direction, spatial layers, and source colors.
3. Compose a vertical 2:3 work: top 38–42% photographic evidence, middle 25–28% symbolic imprint, bottom 30–35% Monet Memory.
4. Keep the top faithful and photographic. Compress 2–4 source relationships into a sparse, asymmetrical, imperfectly printed middle symbol with 55–70% exposed paper and no original perspective.
5. Rebuild 4–7 source fragments in the bottom as a new luminous composition. Preserve the dominant subject's weight and source color identity while changing framing, placement, scale, depth, and relationships.
6. Add a narrow aged-paper information field and a large imperfect circular postmark across its boundary. Bottom and postmark typography are English only; the postmark icon comes from the current photo.
7. Return exactly one finished image without analysis, alternatives, logos, signatures, watermarks, or mockup framing.

## Guardrails

- Never invent or silently increase location specificity. Fame, atmosphere, and style are not geographic proof; a model-generated candidate cannot become evidence for itself.
- Do not add generic suns, moons, pagodas, pavilions, mountains, landmarks, or reusable emblems unless visible in the source.
- Preserve at least three source color groups when available; never impose a stock vintage palette.
- Keep 80–90% clean and breathable. Concentrate aging at selected seams, edges, folds, and stamps.
- The top is not regenerated scenery; the middle is not a traced miniature; the bottom is not an oil-paint filter or a copy of a specific Monet painting.
